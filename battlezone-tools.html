Battlezone Tools

Standalone Python utilities · authoring · diagnostics · extraction · publishing

Standalone Battlezone utilities I maintain outside the larger engine and mod projects. These tools target practical modding workflows: terrain and world authoring, asset conversion, localization, publishing, archive inspection, reverse-engineering extraction, and multiplayer support.
